Changelog

  • Revision 34: Introduced custom font importing. You can now load fonts directly from Google Fonts by name or upload .ttf/.otf files from your device.
  • Revision 35: Added support for font weights in custom fonts. Google Fonts automatically load 400-700 weights, while disk fonts support specific weight files.
  • Revision 36: Implemented persistent storage. All imported custom fonts are now saved to your browser's local storage and will be available every time you return.
  • Revision 41: Enhanced the text rendering engine to ensure perfect vertical centering for all font types and character sets.
  • Revision 43: Added a dedicated symbols toggle for special character support and moved all configuration toggles into a new "Config" tab for a more organized mobile interface.
  • Revision 45: Integrated the Websim Comments API, allowing you to instantly share your creations by posting them directly to the project's comment section with a single click.
